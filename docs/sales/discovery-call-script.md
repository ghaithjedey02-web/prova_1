# Discovery Call — Script & Qualification

**Duration:** 25 minutes. Never run over.
**Objective:** not to sell the implementation. To sell the €2,900 audit, and to
learn enough to know whether they should buy it.

**The ratio:** they talk 70%, we talk 30%. A discovery call where we did most of
the talking is a failed call regardless of how it felt.

---

## Before the call (10 minutes)

- [ ] Read the company's briefing sheet in Notion → Companies
- [ ] Re-read the **"Do NOT Claim"** field
- [ ] Have the demo running in a terminal, already open
- [ ] Have a blank note ready with the six questions below

## Opening (2 min)

> "Grazie del tempo. Le propongo di fare così: le faccio cinque o sei domande
> sul vostro processo di preventivazione, poi le mostro in cinque minuti cosa
> abbiamo costruito. Se a metà strada capisce che non fa per voi, me lo dica e
> chiudiamo prima — non mi offendo."

That last clause raises answer quality immediately. It signals we are here to
find out whether there is a fit, not to push through to a close.

## The six questions (12 min)

These are the entire commercial substance of the call.

**1. "Quante richieste di offerta vi arrivano in una settimana, più o meno?"**
→ Tests **A1**. Below 10/week the economics likely do not work — and we should say so.

**2. "Chi le prepara materialmente?"**
→ Identifies the real user and the bottleneck. If the answer is "io" (the owner),
the pain is personal and the sale is faster.

**3. "Quanto tempo ci vuole per un preventivo, dall'apertura dell'email all'invio?"**
→ The baseline. Write down their exact words.

**4. "E da quando arriva la richiesta a quando parte l'offerta, quanto passa di solito?"**
→ Different question from #3, and the gap between them *is the opportunity*.
Twenty minutes of work spread over four days is the shape of this problem.

**5. "Vi capita di sapere che avete perso un lavoro perché avete risposto tardi?"**
→ Tests **A2** — the single most important assumption in the business. Let the
silence sit. Do not rescue it.

**6. "Quando arriva una richiesta simile a una già fatta, come fate a ritrovare il prezzo di allora?"**
→ Tests **A6**. If the answer is "me lo ricordo" or "cerco nelle cartelle", the
comparables feature — the strongest moment of the demo — is directly relevant.
If they have a clean structured history, the build is easier and cheaper.

### Follow-ups worth having ready
- "Che gestionale usate?" (A7)
- "Chi decide su un investimento di questo tipo?" (A8)
- "Cosa succede oggi se [la persona] è in ferie?"

### Do not ask
- ❌ "Quali sono le vostre sfide?" — vague, invites a vague answer
- ❌ "Siete interessati all'intelligenza artificiale?" — wrong frame entirely
- ❌ Anything answerable from their website. It signals we did not look.

## Demo (6 min)

> "Le mostro cosa succede a cinque email arrivate in una mattina."

Run it. **Stay quiet while it scrolls.** Call out only the three moments:

1. The historic comparable, with the date and whether it was won
2. **The refusal** — *"Poteva inventare un numero qui. Non lo fa."*
3. The cross-customer warning

Then the ask that matters:

> "Se vuole, me ne inoltri una vostra — anche vecchia, anche già evasa. La
> facciamo passare adesso."

## Close (3 min)

**If the pain is real:**

> "Da quello che mi ha detto, il passo sensato è l'Audit di Processo. Dieci
> giorni lavorativi, €2.900. Analizziamo le vostre ultime 50 richieste vere e le
> diamo dei numeri suoi, non medie di settore. Se poi procediamo, i €2.900 se li
> riprende interi.
>
> Se dopo l'audit i numeri dicono che non conviene, glielo diciamo e ci fermiamo lì."

**If they are not qualified — say so:**

> "Onestamente, con [X richieste a settimana / il vostro processo già così
> rapido] non credo che il risparmio giustifichi la spesa. Preferisco dirglielo
> adesso che farle spendere €2.900 per scoprirlo."

Disqualifying honestly costs one deal and buys a reputation. In a province where
the owners all know each other, that trade is heavily in our favour.

## After (within 2 hours)

- [ ] Send the post-meeting email with the process played back to them
- [ ] Log an Activity in Notion with **What We Learned**
- [ ] Update the assumption register — this is the point of the calls
- [ ] Upgrade the hypothesis label from `EVIDENCE-BASED HYPOTHESIS` to
      `CONFIRMED` for anything they actually told us

## Qualification scorecard

| Signal | Qualified | Disqualified |
|---|---|---|
| RFQ volume | ≥10/week | <5/week |
| Response time | Days | Same day, consistently |
| Quoting owner | 1–3 named people | Fully automated already |
| Lost orders to slowness | Can recall examples | Never happens |
| Quote history | Exists, even messily | None at all |
| Decision | Owner decides | Group/committee, long cycle |
| Size | 10–80 employees | <8 or >150 |

**Four or more "disqualified" → do not sell them the audit.** Mark
`Squalificato` in Notion with the reason. That record is still valuable: the
pattern of who disqualifies is how the targeting improves.
